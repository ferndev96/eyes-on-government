Eyes On Government

Eyes On Government is an interactive forum which allows readers and viewers to engage in the political process.

Our Mission: To make government accountable to the people by helping everyday people to understand and impact the political process.

Covering controversial elections and issues while providing a path for the general public to become informed on the voting process.

Eyes on Government is you and your neighbor banning together to effect positive change in government. You are the people on whom the burden lies to keep government working for the people by keeping your Eyes on Government.

Our work honors our mentor and predecessor Ray Pearson who started the Ray Pearson Forums in the early 70’s.
